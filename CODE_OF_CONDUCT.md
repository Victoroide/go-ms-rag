# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the change, not the person proposing it.
- Explain objections with examples and offer a way forward.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles.
- Respect privacy and accept requests to stop unwanted contact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual remarks, targeted attacks and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not share another person's private
information or continue contacting someone after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Use the contact details on Victoroide's GitHub profile for a private report.
If the incident includes a security risk, use the private advisory channel
described in SECURITY.md. Include the relevant discussion, what happened and
whether there is an immediate safety concern. Do not repeat private details in
public discussions.

## Enforcement

Victoroide reviews reports and may remove content, request a correction, issue a
warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response
depends on the severity, prior behavior and risk to others. Report details are
shared only with those needed to address the incident. A person subject to a
restriction may request a review through the same private reporting channel.
